Loona Edits — Frequently Asked Questions

Loona Edits · Loona Automation

Is Loona Edits really free, with no watermark?

Yes. Loona Edits is free, and exported videos carry no Loona Edits watermark. You may optionally add the Loona logo or your own logo as a watermark in the Brand tab, at the size, corner and opacity you choose — that choice is yours, not a condition.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. There is no account, no sign-up and nothing to install. Open the page, add your clips and edit. Projects you save are stored in this browser on this device.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Unlike Loona's AI services, Loona Edits sends nothing to the Loona Engine or any server. Your video, photo and audio files are processed entirely in your browser on your device, and the export is rendered locally as well. Your media never leaves your device through this tool.

Which export format will I get?

MP4 where your browser supports recording it, otherwise WebM — the editor tells you which one your browser will produce before you export. WebM plays in all modern players and uploads to social platforms without problems. If export is not supported in your browser at all, the editor will say so and recommend Chrome.

What can I make with Loona Edits?

Short-form and everyday videos in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 and 4:5: trimmed and split clips, photo slideshows with a slow zoom, titles, burned-in captions (typed or imported from an .SRT file), music and voice tracks with fades, filters, transitions, intro and end cards, a logo watermark and a name strip.

Where did my saved project go?

Saved projects live in this browser's storage on this device. They will not appear in a different browser or after browser data is cleared, and there is no cloud backup. Export your finished video and keep the file somewhere safe.

What can Loona Edits not do?

It is not a professional desktop suite: there is no multi-camera editing, no effects library and no cloud rendering. Because rendering happens on your device, very long or high-resolution projects can be slow on older phones, and large files can strain memory. Keep projects focused and export in parts if a device struggles.
