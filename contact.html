The fastest way to reach the team behind Loona Edits is by email: loonaautomation@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 working days. Please put Loona Edits in your subject line, and tell us your browser and device, your chosen canvas size, and roughly how long your project is — because everything renders on your device, those three facts decide almost every export question.

What to write to us about

Write to us about exports that fail or look wrong, captions that land at the wrong time, features you need for the videos you actually make (a new canvas size, a transition, a text style), or using Loona Edits for your school, shop or channel. If a video you edited with Loona Edits is published publicly, we would love to see the link.

Try these first — common Loona Edits fixes

The editor says export is not supported. Export renders with your browser's own recording tools, and not every browser offers them: switch to Chrome, where export works fully. You got WebM instead of MP4. That is your browser's limit, not an error — WebM plays in all modern players and uploads to social platforms fine. Export is very slow or the tab struggles. Rendering happens on your device, so close other tabs, shorten the project, and on an older phone try a smaller canvas size. Your saved project is missing. Projects live in this browser on this device only: opening Loona Edits in a different browser or after clearing browser data will not show them — export finished videos promptly and keep the files. Captions are evenly spread but you need exact timings. Import an .SRT file instead; SRT captions keep their own timings exactly. The music is missing from the export. Check the Audio tab: the music track must be added there, with its volume above zero and its start time inside the video.
