About Loona Edits

Loona Edits · Loona Automation

Loona Edits is a complete video editor that never asks you to upload your video anywhere — every cut, caption and export happens inside your own browser, on your own device.

An editor built for the videos people actually post

Loona Edits starts where most short-video work starts: the canvas. You can build in 9:16 Vertical for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, 16:9 Wide, 1:1 Square or 4:5 Portrait, and switch sizes freely. Add videos, or add photos and they become slideshow clips with a slow Ken Burns zoom. One tap adds a branded intro card or end card using Loona branding automatically. When you tap Export, everything on the timeline — titles, captions, logo and music — is rendered into a single video file, with no watermark, free.

Inside the editor, tab by tab

The Timeline lets you split a clip at the playhead, duplicate it or delete it, and tapping any clip selects it for editing. Clip controls cover trimming and playback speed. Text offers Big Hook, Caption Bar and Simple title styles that you can drag around the preview to position. Captions can be typed line by line and spread evenly across the video, or imported from an .SRT file so your exact timings are kept, with control over position, size and a dark backing bar. Audio gives you separate music and voice-recording tracks, each with volume, fade in, fade out and a start offset, plus a mute switch for the sound recorded inside your clips. Look applies filters — Warm, Cool, Vivid, Black & White, Vintage — with brightness and contrast sliders and transitions between clips: cut, fade through black, or dissolve. Brand adds the Loona logo watermark (or your own logo file) in any corner at your chosen size and opacity, fills empty space with a blurred copy of the clip or a solid colour, and can show a name strip — a lower-third with your name and a line below it.

How export works — and why nothing is uploaded

Unlike the AI services in the Loona family, Loona Edits does not send your work to the Loona Engine or to any server. Your video, photo and audio files are read locally, the preview is drawn on a canvas in the page, and the export is recorded in the browser itself: MP4 where your browser supports it, otherwise WebM. Projects you save are stored in this browser on this device. Closing the page loses nothing you saved, but clearing browser data or changing devices removes your projects, and there is no cloud backup — keep exported files safe yourself.

Limits, stated plainly

Because rendering happens on your device, export speed depends on your phone or computer: long or high-resolution projects render slowly on older hardware, and very large files can strain a phone's memory. Export is not supported in every browser — the editor itself will tell you, and recommends Chrome. Loona Edits is a fast, practical editor for short-form and everyday video, not a replacement for a professional desktop suite with multi-camera timelines and effects libraries.

Who it is for

Teachers turning lessons into watchable videos, small businesses making product clips, creators cutting Reels and Shorts, and anyone who has ever abandoned a video because another editor demanded an upload, an account or a payment before removing its watermark. Here there is no account, no upload and no watermark — the door is simply open.
